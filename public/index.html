Questions people ask

What does Riva Computech do?

Riva Computech provides IT support for small businesses. That covers Microsoft 365 and email, sign-ins and access, moving to new systems, backups, device and email security, servers and networks, and PowerShell automation.

Who will I deal with?

Devik Patel, the owner. He scopes the job, does the work and answers your messages. There is no help desk queue and no hand-off to someone else.

What does it cost?

The first 30-minute chat is free. After that you get a plan that says what needs to be done, in what order, and what it will cost, before any work starts.

When is Riva Computech available?

Weekdays, evenings, weekends and holidays. Messages usually get a reply within one business day.

Do you take one-off jobs, or only ongoing support?

Both. A one-off job might be a migration, a new Microsoft 365 setup, a clean-up or a rebuild. Ongoing support means regular monitoring and maintenance, with a direct line when something breaks.

Is Riva Computech the right fit for my business?

It suits small businesses that want one person who knows their setup and answers their messages. If you need a staffed help desk around the clock, a larger provider will suit you better.

Which technologies do you work with?

Mostly Microsoft: Microsoft 365, Exchange Online, SharePoint, OneDrive, Intune, Entra ID, Active Directory, Azure and Windows Server. Also VMware, Hyper-V and Citrix, plus firewalls, VPNs, DNS and NAS storage.

Is Riva Computech part of Microsoft?

No. Riva Computech is an independent business. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, VMware or Citrix.

Does Microsoft back up my Microsoft 365 data?

Only up to a point. Microsoft keeps the service running and holds deleted items for a limited time. Getting older data back, or recovering after a ransomware attack, is largely up to you. A separate backup of mail, OneDrive and SharePoint covers that gap.

Microsoft calls this split shared responsibility.

What is MFA, and do I need it?

MFA (multi-factor authentication) means signing in with a second step, such as a code on your phone, as well as your password. A 2023 Microsoft study found that it reduced the risk of an account being compromised by more than 99%. It is worth turning on for every account, including admins.

Source: Microsoft Research, May 2023.

What is a Microsoft 365 tenant?

A tenant is your organization's own Microsoft 365 account: its users, email, files and settings. A tenant-to-tenant migration moves all of that from one tenant to another, for example after a merger.
