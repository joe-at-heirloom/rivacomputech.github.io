What we can help with

Everyday computer and email help for your business. A one-off job, or ongoing support.

  • Email and Microsoft 365

    We set up and look after your work email, Word, Excel, Teams and shared files, so everything is tidy and safe.

  • Sign-ins and passwords

    One secure sign-in for each person, with access to the right things. Help when someone joins, leaves or gets locked out.

  • Moving to a new system

    Changing email provider or moving your files? We plan it carefully, so nothing is lost and work carries on.

  • Backups

    Safe copies of your email and files. We test them, so we know they can be brought back on a bad day.

  • Protection from scams and viruses

    We keep your computers, phones and email protected, and watch for anything suspicious.

  • Servers and office networks

    Office servers, networks and secure connections for working from home. Set up properly and kept running.

We can also save you time. Jobs you repeat on the computer can be made to happen automatically, the same way every time, so nothing gets forgotten.
